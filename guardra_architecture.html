How Guardra Protects You in 4 Simple Steps

Guardra runs silently inside your browser to block annoying ads, prevent secret tracking, grade company privacy policies with AI, and help you legally delete your personal data.

⚡
Step 1 • Live Browsing

Kills Ads & Hidden Trackers

As you browse, Guardra's high-speed engine intercepts video pre-rolls on YouTube, hides sponsored posts on Instagram/Facebook feeds, and stops 100+ ad networks from following you.

  • ✓ Instant YouTube video ad auto-skip (<0.05s)
  • ✓ Removes sponsored posts on Instagram & Facebook
  • ✓ Blocks Google, Meta, TikTok, and Taboola trackers
🏷️
Step 2 • On-Page Pill

Always-On Privacy Pill

A sleek, unobtrusive floating pill sits at the bottom of web pages showing live blocked ads and active tracking cookies, matching your browser's light or dark mode in real time.

  • ✓ Live counter: 🛡️ 12 Ads Blocked
  • ✓ Real-time Light / Dark theme synchronization
  • ✓ 1-Click to expand full in-page security audit
🧠
Step 3 • AI Analysis

AI Policy Score (Grade A - F)

Nobody reads 20-page legal terms. Guardra's AI scans the website's privacy policy in 0.2 seconds, checks whether they sell your data or retain it forever, and assigns a simple letter score.

  • ✓ 6-Pillar rating: Data Sharing, Retention, Security
  • ✓ DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR compliance check
  • ✓ Instant Grievance Officer contact finder
✉️
Step 4 • Take Control

1-Click Legal Data Deletion

Want a company to erase your phone number, address, and purchase history? Guardra generates and sends a statutory DPDP Sec 12 / GDPR data deletion notice to their legal desk with 1 click.

  • ✓ Legally binding statutory deletion demand
  • ✓ Alerts if the site had verified dark web data breaches
  • ✓ 1-Click cookie neutralizer for advertising cookies
